    My Philosophy

    Good Enough is Never Enough.

    My coding journey started with curiosity and a desire to build something meaningful. At first, everything felt unfamiliar new terms, new rules, new ways of thinking. Simple lines of code looked confusing, and errors felt frustrating. But instead of quitting, I leaned into the discomfort. As I began learning website design, I realized coding isn’t just about writing commands it’s about problem-solving, creativity, and patience. Every small win, from fixing a bug to creating a simple layout, gave me confidence. Each challenge taught me how to think logically and approach problems step by step. There were moments I doubted myself, but I reminded myself why I started: to grow, to learn a valuable skill, and to create opportunities for my future. With consistency and practice, things that once seemed impossible started making sense. My coding journey is still ongoing, and I’m proud of how far I’ve come. Every day, I’m learning, improving, and getting closer to becoming the developer I envision. This is just the beginning.
